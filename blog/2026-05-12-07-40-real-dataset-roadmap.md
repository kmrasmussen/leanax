# Real Dataset Training Roadmap

The next suite moves from artifact availability to command-facing classifier
training.

`TICKET-0050` makes the derived-mask train-step the command-facing artifact.
`TICKET-0051` adds a bounded cached real-dataset training sweep.
`TICKET-0052` records structured metrics for that sweep. `TICKET-0053` updates
the readiness report. `TICKET-0054` writes down the direct MNIST runtime
boundary before expanding that harder track.
