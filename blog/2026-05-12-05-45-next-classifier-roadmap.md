# Next Classifier Roadmap

The completed `TICKET-0027` through `TICKET-0035` suite got LeanAX to a checked
ten-class fixture classifier path: generated forward, loss, gradient, update,
IDX loader, fixture training, and a strict progress report.

The next queue starts at `TICKET-0036` and focuses on the remaining honest gaps:
one monolithic train-step artifact, a user-facing train command, cached
full-dataset smoke metrics, runtime capability reporting, denser runtime
fixtures, and a second readiness-report closeout.

The roadmap now names those phases directly so future implementation can choose
small slices without losing the larger classifier story.
